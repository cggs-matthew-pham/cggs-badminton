                 House Badminton
Rules:
•   Full court is used during play.
•   Rally point scoring – if you win a rally you gain a point and the right to serve.
•   Players must rotate between their own servers.
•   Service is from anywhere behind the front service line.
•   A true serve must land on or beyond the front service line on the other side.
•   Underarm service action is required.
•   When serving the racquet must hit the shuttle at waist height or lower.
•   The winner is the team with the highest score at the end of the match.
                 House Badminton
A fault occurs if:
• a serve hits the net.
• if the server attempting to serve misses the shuttle twice.
• if in play the shuttle lands outside the court or passes through or under the net.
• the shuttle touches any other object other then player’s racquet or net.
• the shuttle is not hit on the strikers side of the net.
• the person touches the net with racquet or person.
• the player slings or hits the shuttle twice in succession except with one stroke.
• the shuttle hits any part of the ceiling or bounces off a wall.
