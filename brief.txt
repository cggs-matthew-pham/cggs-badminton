HOUSE WATER VOLLEYBALL RULES 

 

 

Rules:   

 

Paper/scissors/rock to see who starts the first serve  
  

Rally point – if you win the point, you win the right to serve in the next point 

 

Players may set, dig or tap the ball, no double hits  

 

Overarm serve, the ball must clear the net, otherwise, point and serve goes to the opposite team  

 

Players must not touch the net at any time  

 

The ball must be touched at least once on each side of the net during a rally  

 

If the ball is hit out of the pool on the full, the point and serve are given to the opposite team  

 

Once the ball hits the water the rally ends and the point and serve go to the attacking team  

 

Blood rule applies  

 

The team with the most rally points is declared the winner at the end of 10 mins play

HOUSE WATERPOLO RULES 

 

Rules:   

 

Each team has 7 players in the pool, including the goal keeper. 

 

At the start of play, the players line up (stationary kicking) on their respective goal line. The whistle is blown and the ball dropped on the half way line available for both teams. 

 

A goal may be scored by the ball passing fully over the goal line, between the goal posts. A goal may be scored by any part of the body except the clenched fist. Dribbling the ball through the posts is acceptable. 

 

No corner throws, goal throws allowed after a shot at goal has been taken. 

 

Fouls are taken as free passes. 

 

Ordinary fouls include:  

 

to push off the posts or sides or bottom of the pool 

to take or hold the ball under the water when tackled 

to strike at the ball with a clenched fist 

to impede or prevent the free movement of a player unless they are holding the ball 

to touch the ball with both hands at the same time (one hand throw and catch) 

to push, or push off from an opponent 

to be within 2 metres of the opponents goal line unless the ball is there. 

 

No personal, ejection or dead time fouls, or penalty throws. 

 

Blood rule applies.  

 

 

(Note: Use discretion with beginning players – 2 handed catch may be allowed) 

 

All teams must have a min of 5 players in the pool. Houses should move students from other sports to ensure teams are full prior to the day.

HOUSE NETBALL RULES
